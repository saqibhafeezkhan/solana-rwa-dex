# Risk and Compliance Model

## Core risk concerns

- Price risk for RWA-backed instruments
- Liquidity risk during stressed periods
- Counterparty and custody risk
- Regulatory and jurisdictional risk
- Funding and settlement risk
- Smart contract security risk

## Controls to design

- Whitelist or eligibility checks
- Maximum exposure per asset class
- Position limits by wallet or institution
- Role-based market admin permissions
- Oracle freshness checks
- Slippage and price deviation protections
- Event notifications for suspicious activity

## Compliance strategy

Compliance should be integrated from the beginning, even if initial usage is limited to a controlled environment. This is essential because RWA trading is structurally more regulated than generic crypto trading.
